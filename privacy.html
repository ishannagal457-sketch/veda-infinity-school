Privacy Policy

Last updated · 15 March 2025

1. Introduction

Veda Infinity School ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of every parent, student, and visitor. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website vedainfinity.school.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you fill out our admission enquiry form or Transfer Certificate request form:

  • Parent's or guardian's name
  • Student's name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Grade of interest or current grade
  • Roll number, section, and academic year (for TC requests)
  • Any message or reason you choose to share with us

We also collect anonymous analytics data (page views, browser type, device type) through Google Analytics to improve our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to:

  • Contact you regarding your admission enquiry
  • Arrange a campus visit if requested
  • Process your Transfer Certificate request
  • Send you admission-related updates (only if you request them)
  • Improve our website and services

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Photographs of Students

Photographs of students published on this website are used only after obtaining written consent from parents or guardians. If you wish to withdraw consent for your child's photograph, please email privacy@vedainfinity.school and we will remove it within 7 working days.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Our website uses SSL encryption (HTTPS). Form submissions are transmitted securely and stored on Formspree's servers with industry-standard security.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics, which places cookies on your device to collect anonymous usage data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to browse our site.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for communication at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@vedainfinity.school.

8. Children's Privacy

We comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India). We collect information about children only through their parents or legal guardians and only for admission or administrative purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
